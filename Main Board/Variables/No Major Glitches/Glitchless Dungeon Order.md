## Goal: Glitchless Dungeon Order
1. Timing starts on file select and ends upon entering the Triforce room.
2. **Save & Quit is banned**. Intentionally dying outside of a dungeon to emulate a Save & Quit is banned. Similarly, console resets (soft and hard) are banned.
3. You must complete the Dark World dungeons in the order listed 1-7. Entering any other Dark World dungeon but the one you are currently on is disallowed.
4. This category follows the Glitchless ruleset.

## Glitchless
(It is recommended to use the JP 1.2 or US versions of the game for runs under this ruleset as those versions patch out many of the glitches under the "Banned" list below)

**Allowed**
- Pumping
- Stair Drag Cancel
- Abusing i-frames (e.g. big key damage boost in Eastern)
- Interior Rail Clipping (e.g. clipping through the freestanding rail in Hera petting zoo)
- Bastard Door Early Item Drop

**Banned**
- All glitches not allowed in the NMG ruleset
- Bomb Jumps
- Silverless Ganon
- Torch Glitch
- Intentional Houlihan
- Item Dashing
- Superspeed (Armed or Active)
- Block Push Glitches (e.g. Hook/Fire Rod through block, Mirror Block Deletion)
- Big Bomb Dupe
- Big Bomb/Purple Chest Dashing
- Hovering
- Fake Flippers
- Waterwalk
- Damage Overriding with Fire Rod doubles on Kholdstare
- Forcing Enemies Off Screen or Out of Bounds (e.g. Mimic clipping)
- Spooky Action at a Distance with large altitude displacement (e.g. hitting switches with bomb spooky action in Swamp or firerod spooky action in Mire)
- Ancilla Overloading
- Fake Items (Powder, Bow, Mirror, etc.)
- Intentional Heart/Fairy Dupe (i.e. equipping boom/hook to intentionally dupe an accessible drop)
- Slippery Stair Clip
- Bonk Prize Pre-Grab
- Boomerange
- Medallion Cancel
- Magic Cape Cheapskate
- Magic Overflow
- LSD Blind & Variants
- Remote Purple Chest
- Sanc & Quit
- Intentional Prize on the Eyes
- All Bunny Glitches listed in the NMG ruleset
